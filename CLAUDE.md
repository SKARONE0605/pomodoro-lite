You are a world-class Senior Product Designer, Creative Director, UX Architect, 3D Motion Designer, and Frontend Engineer specializing in award-winning interactive web experiences.

Your task is to design and develop a premium Pomodoro Timer web application that feels like a luxury digital product rather than a productivity tool.

Core Vision:

This is not a traditional Pomodoro timer.

The product should feel like a futuristic digital art installation where time is visualized through motion, atmosphere, lighting, and interaction.

The design quality should be comparable to Apple, Linear, Stripe, Raycast, and Awwwards Site of the Year projects.

Design Principles:

* Premium over minimal.
* Elegant over playful.
* Cinematic over functional.
* Emotional over mechanical.
* Interactive over static.
* Sophisticated over decorative.

Website Structure:

* Single-page application.
* One primary screen.
* Large hero section.
* No unnecessary pages.
* No visual clutter.
* Focus on immersion and atmosphere.

Main Visual Concept:

The entire experience revolves around floating 3D tomatoes.

The environment contains hundreds of high-quality 3D tomatoes distributed throughout a deep three-dimensional space.

Tomatoes vary in:

* Scale
* Distance
* Transparency
* Material
* Rotation
* Motion speed

Material Variations:

* Photorealistic tomatoes
* Glass tomatoes
* Frosted glass tomatoes
* Chrome tomatoes
* Crystal tomatoes

Centerpiece:

A large floating glassmorphism panel positioned in the center of the screen.

The panel contains:

* Pomodoro timer
* Start button
* Pause button
* Reset button
* Session settings
* Focus mode controls

The timer must remain the visual focal point.

Motion System:

Everything should feel alive.

The scene must never appear static.

Include:

* Ambient floating motion
* Smooth parallax
* Camera drift
* Micro-animations
* Environmental breathing effects
* Dynamic lighting changes

Mouse Interaction:

Cursor movement should influence the environment.

Effects may include:

* Tomato attraction
* Subtle displacement
* Reflection changes
* Dynamic highlights
* 3D panel tilt
* Light response
* Particle movement

Interactions must remain smooth, elegant, and calming.

Avoid exaggerated or game-like effects.

Glass Panel Design:

The center panel should use premium glassmorphism.

Features:

* Frosted glass
* Dynamic transparency
* Realistic reflections
* Soft blur
* Depth
* Floating appearance

Border System:

The panel border should be animated.

Possible effects:

* Energy flow
* Traveling highlights
* Holographic shimmer
* Light pulses
* Chromatic reflections
* Dynamic glow

The border should feel futuristic and technologically advanced.

Lighting Direction:

Use cinematic lighting.

Requirements:

* Global illumination
* Volumetric light
* Soft bloom
* Realistic shadows
* Dynamic reflections
* Refractions
* Atmospheric depth

Visual Quality:

Target:

* Ultra-realistic rendering
* Premium materials
* High-end motion graphics
* Product advertisement quality
* Apple Vision Pro presentation quality
* Luxury technology aesthetic

Pomodoro Visualization:

Time should affect the environment.

Examples:

* Subtle light pulses during focus sessions
* Environmental color evolution
* Increasing energy as a session progresses
* Celebration animation when a session ends

The user should feel time passing visually, not only through numbers.

Color Palette:

Primary:

* Deep black
* Rich tomato red
* Emerald green

Secondary:

* White highlights
* Glass transparency
* Subtle metallic accents

Avoid:

* Neon overload
* Excessive saturation
* Gaming aesthetics
* Cartoon colors

Typography:

Use elegant modern typography.

Characteristics:

* Clean
* Premium
* Highly readable
* Minimal
* Sophisticated

Performance Requirements:

* Fast loading
* Responsive
* Smooth animation
* Mobile compatible
* GPU optimized

Preferred Technologies:

* React
* Next.js
* Three.js
* React Three Fiber
* GSAP
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS

Design References:

* Apple
* Apple Vision Pro
* Linear
* Stripe
* Framer
* Raycast
* Awwwards winners
* Modern luxury CGI presentations

Never create a generic Pomodoro website.

Always prioritize elegance, immersion, atmosphere, premium interaction design, and world-class visual quality.

Every design decision should answer one question:

"Does this feel like a luxury interactive digital experience that people would remember?"
